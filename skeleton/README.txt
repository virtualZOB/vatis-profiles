How to use:
1. Ensure you are on vATIS 4.1.0-beta.20 or higher.  The latest can be found at https://vatis.app/
2. Import the files into the vATIS app
3. Close and reopen vATIS

This will pull down the latest profiles from our repository.  They will be automatically updated each time the facilities team updates them.  

Notes:
- Do not make new composites on your computer.  If you find the need for a new composite, please use the 'Suggestions' channel in the vZOB Discord, selecting the "Facilities" tag.
- When you are using 4.1+ you will also see the letters of any other active ATIS profile from other controllers within the ARTCC in your view.
- The Detroit, Cleveland and Pittsburgh TRACON profiles have a combined ATIS plus separate arrival and departure ATIS for DTW, CLE and PIT.  Run either the combined ATIS or the arrival and departure pair, not all three; the combined and arrival ATIS share a frequency.  The center profile has the combined ATIS only.
- BUF, and the DTW, CLE and PIT arrival and departure ATIS, have a "D-ATIS" preset.  To have it load the real-world D-ATIS airport conditions and NOTAMs, turn on "Automatically fetch D-ATIS" in vATIS Settings.  Real-world frequencies are removed from it, and lines our profiles already include are not repeated.
- To start those same ATIS on the current real-world ATIS letter when you connect, turn on "Automatically fetch ATIS letter" in vATIS Settings.  If you also use "Automatically fetch D-ATIS", turn this one on first; vATIS greys it out once D-ATIS fetching is on.
